About niroNews

niroNews is the cinema news arm of niroMovie, built to keep our audience up to date with what's happening in film and television — new releases, trending titles, behind‑the‑scenes stories, and the conversations fans are having about them.

We work alongside niroMovie, our streaming and movie‑discovery platform, to bring readers a single place to follow both the stories behind the screen and the movies themselves. Where niroMovie helps you find what to watch, niroNews helps you understand what's happening in the industry around it.

niroNews is founded and maintained by Niyonkuru Robert, who leads the editorial direction and product behind both niroNews and niroMovie.
