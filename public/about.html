About niroNews

niroNews is the official cinema news portal of niroMovie, your gateway to unlimited entertainment. We bring the latest updates about movies, series, and the entertainment world, with a special focus on African and global cinema.

Founded by Niyonkuru Robert, we are committed to keeping Rwandan and global audiences informed. Our team scours the internet to find trending stories, release dates, and exclusive behind‑the‑scenes content that matters to movie lovers.

This site is funded through advertising and sponsorships. We strive to maintain editorial independence and only publish content that we believe is accurate and valuable.
