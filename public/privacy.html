Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal data necessary to provide our services:

- A device identifier (random string stored in your browser) to enable the "like" feature without requiring an account. This identifier cannot identify you personally.

- The name you provide when posting a comment (optional). You may use a pseudonym.

2. How We Use Your Data

- The device ID is used solely to remember which news articles you have liked, so you cannot like the same article multiple times.

- Comment names are displayed publicly alongside your comment.

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties. No advertising networks receive your comment name or device ID.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics. The only data stored in your browser is the device ID mentioned above, which is used for functional purposes only.

4. Third‑Party Services

We display advertisements through Monetag (PropellerAds). Monetag may use its own cookies and tracking technologies. Please refer to Monetag's privacy policy for details.

We also fetch movie data from The Movie Database (TMDB) for display purposes. No personal data is sent to TMDB.

5. Your Rights

You can clear your browser's site data at any time to reset your device ID. If you wish to have a comment removed, please contact us at news@niromovie.site and we will delete it promptly.

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, email news@niromovie.site.
