Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We only collect a device identifier (a random string stored in your browser) to allow you to like articles without logging in. When you post a comment, you may provide a name, which will be displayed publicly alongside your comment. No other personal data is collected.

2. How We Use Your Data

The device ID is used solely to remember which articles you have liked, so you cannot like the same article multiple times. The name you provide with a comment is shown publicly next to your comment. We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies. The only data stored in your browser is the device ID mentioned above, which is used for functional purposes only.

4. Third‑Party Services

We display advertisements through Monetag (PropellerAds). Monetag may use its own cookies and tracking technologies – please refer to their privacy policy for details. We also fetch movie data from The Movie Database (TMDB) for display purposes; no personal data is sent to TMDB.

5. Your Rights

You can clear your browser’s site data at any time to reset your device ID. To request removal of a comment, please contact us at news@niromovie.site and we will delete it promptly.

6. Contact

For privacy concerns, email news@niromovie.site.
